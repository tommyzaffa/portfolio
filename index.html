About

I grew up between a guitar, a camera and a keyboard and I still haven't picked one.

Two years at CISA film school and a degree in Visual Design. Then I went back to maths and enrolled in Computer Science at USI, with a semester at Sapienza in Rome.

Right now I'm CTO at PoPaCi and Creative Technologist at the Merge Film Festival, the first Swiss festival for films made with AI. The rest of the time I build websites for restaurants, tattoo studios and fashion boutiques, and I release music with a couple of bands.

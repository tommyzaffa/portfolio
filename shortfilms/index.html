Short Films

Here is a selection of the short films and audiovisual projects I have created, from my high school years to my time at CISA film school, along with the corresponding links.


The Art of Letting Go

The Art of Letting Go is my first professional independent short film made entirely with artificial intelligence.

A first date. Dinner, a walk, a rose, a kiss. Nothing out of the ordinary — depending on where you draw the line.



In the Depths of the Sea

This was my first time using AI image and video generation, and I used it to create a music video for the very first song I ever released on streaming platforms: In the Depths of the Sea, from my 2023 album To the Castle!. Ever since the 2022 single cover, I had dreamed of an animated video in an impressionist style to go with it — and with AI I was finally able to bring that dream to life.



CISA

At CISA, I created several short films for various contests, which gave me invaluable experience in directing and exposure to other areas of filmmaking, such as sound and editing.

Please note that the short films I produced at CISA are under the institution’s copyright; therefore, the link below refers only to a backstage video.



Io Lui e l'Altro

While in high school, I created a production called Io Lui e l'Altro, through which I published numerous short films on YouTube. I handled most of the production myself, with friends pitching in as actors. This experience taught me a great deal about directing, coordinating with a team, and bringing creative projects to life.

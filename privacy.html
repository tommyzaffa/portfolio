Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Website owner

This website is operated by Thomas Zaffalon. For privacy-related requests, contact thomas@tommyzaffa.com.

Data processed

This is a static personal portfolio. It does not provide user accounts, online payments, comments, newsletters or advertising profiling. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you browse it, technical data needed to serve and secure the website may be processed by the hosting provider, such as IP address, browser, device, date and time of the request.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, Clarity or similar tracking tools, and it sets no profiling cookies. The only thing it writes to your browser is a single temporary flag in session storage, used to play the transition animation when you move from one page to another. It holds no personal data and your browser discards it when you close the tab.

Third-party services

The site loads the Syne and Inter Tight typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser requests those files from Google and Google receives your IP address in the process. Links to Instagram, LinkedIn, Telegram, YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and other external platforms are simple outbound links; those services may process data under their own policies when you open them.

Contact

If you contact me through email, Telegram or social platforms, the data you provide is used only to read and answer your message. The communication platform you choose may also process data under its own policy.

Your rights

You may request information about personal data concerning you, and ask for correction or deletion where applicable. You can also clear browser data at any time through your browser settings.

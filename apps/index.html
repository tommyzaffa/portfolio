Apps & Websites

Twelve things that are online right now. Restaurants, tattoo studios, a fashion boutique, a film festival, a management platform and a couple of games I built because I wanted to play them. All of it written by hand, and all of it still being tweaked.

  • 01

    Pasta Lovers

    Website Australia Wix integration

    A fresh pasta restaurant in Australia and my first client outside Europe. They were stuck on Wix, so I rebuilt the whole site by hand and plugged their existing ordering page back into it, so nothing changed for the customers or for the staff.

    Live
  • 02

    La Brace

    Website GSAP · Lenis Bilingual

    A steakhouse near Lugano doing dry aged meat over open coals. They wanted to leave WordPress behind, so I rebuilt the site from scratch: a video hero that opens as you scroll, kinetic type, a scroll-driven gallery, and menus the staff update from one data file.

    Live
  • 03

    PoPaCi Studio

    Web App Next.js · TypeScript · Supabase Full-stack

    The internal platform I designed and built for PoPaCi and the Merge Film Festival team, and the biggest thing I've taken on so far. A screenplay editor with PDF export, project boards, a shared calendar, chat with video calls, a collaborative whiteboard and a festival module, all behind invite-only auth with row level security.

    Live
  • 04

    Keyo Tattoo Studio

    Website Brand identity Lugano

    The brief contradicted itself on purpose: keep the studio's identity, but make it feel like a New York tattoo shop. It took a few passes, and the owner seems happy with where we landed.

    Live

    Echo Maze
